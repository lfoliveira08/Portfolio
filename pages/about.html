About me.

Computer science student, security researcher, D1 swimmer, and trilingual human from Brazil living in San Antonio, TX.

I'm Luis and I amcurrently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Information Systems at the University of the Incarnate Word in San Antonio, TX, with a focus on cybersecurity and computer programming.

My academic journey started at Iowa Central Community College in Iowa, where I earned an Associate of Arts in Business/Commerce while swimming competitively. I transferred to UIW to go deeper into security and systems.

At UIW I work as Undergraduate Research Assistant, where I simulate real-world critical infrastructure attacks, build ICS/SCADA testbeds, and conduct vulnerability assessments on embedded systems.

Outside the lab and classroom, I'm the Swim Team Captain and a member of the Student-Athlete Advisory Committee (SAAC). Competing at the D1 level has shaped how I think, consistency, and performing under pressure.

I'm fluent in Portuguese, English, and intermediate Spanish. Originally from Brazil, now building a career in programming andcybersecurity in the US.
